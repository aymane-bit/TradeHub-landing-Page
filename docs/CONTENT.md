## 7. Section-by-section content direction

### 7.1 Header and navigation

The header should be quiet, compact, and product-focused.

Recommended navigation labels:

- Product
- Vision or About
- Founders
- Contact
- Request Early Access

Requirements:

- Use the actual TradeHub logo or wordmark if provided.
- Keep the primary CTA visually distinct.
- Provide a keyboard-accessible mobile menu if a collapsed menu is needed.
- Avoid excessive links, dropdowns, or premature pages.
- Do not imply that users can sign in or trade unless those routes genuinely exist and are intentionally exposed.

### 7.2 Hero

The hero must immediately explain what TradeHub is, where it is focused, that the product exists, and what visitors can do next.

#### Recommended headline

> Investing in Morocco, brought together.

#### Recommended supporting copy

> TradeHub is a Morocco-first investing platform bringing market information, investing practice, portfolio tools, and investor community into one integrated experience.

#### Product-status line

> A working TradeHub platform is already built. We're preparing to open it to early users.

#### CTAs

- Primary: **Request Early Access**
- Secondary: **Explore TradeHub**

#### Short clarity note

> TradeHub currently provides a virtual investing environment. Real-money trade execution is not currently available.

The clarity note must be readable but should not visually dominate the hero.

#### Hero visual

Use authentic TradeHub product screenshots or approved exports from the real application. The main visual should show the dashboard, while supporting crops or adjacent views may reveal the market, portfolio, and community areas.

Do not create fake interfaces, fake balances presented as real data, invented company data, or decorative fintech illustrations. If final assets are unavailable, use neutral labeled placeholders that are easy to replace and cannot be mistaken for real product imagery.

### 7.3 The fragmented investor experience

This section should describe the problem without unsupported market statistics.

Core idea:

> For people exploring investing in Morocco, useful information, practice, portfolio activity, and community discussion can feel separated across different tools and channels.

Potential problem themes:

- information is spread across separate sources;
- practical learning is disconnected from market context;
- portfolio activity is isolated from discussion and shared learning; and
- tools are not always designed around the needs and context of Moroccan retail investors.

Keep the tone constructive. Do not portray Moroccan institutions, brokers, exchanges, or existing services negatively. Do not claim that no solutions exist.

### 7.4 TradeHub's integrated solution

Explain that TradeHub brings the journey into one coherent environment:

- explore market information;
- practice investment decisions virtually;
- follow portfolio positions and performance; and
- discuss companies, markets, ideas, and experiences with a community.

Suggested section idea:

> From market discovery to practice, portfolio understanding, and community conversation—TradeHub brings the experience together around the Moroccan market.

This section should bridge the problem into the product pillars rather than repeat the hero.

### 7.5 Core product pillars

Use four pillars. Keep them concise, balanced, and linked to real product functionality.

#### Market Information

Explore market and company information relevant to the Moroccan investing ecosystem. Do not state or imply redistribution authorization, live exchange affiliation, or official exchange data unless separately verified.

#### Investing Practice

A virtual environment where users can explore investing decisions without real-money execution. Virtual balances, orders, and positions must be described as simulated.

#### Portfolio Tools

Tools for understanding portfolio allocation, performance, positions, and investing activity within the TradeHub experience.

#### Investor Community

Community functionality for discussing companies, markets, investment ideas, and investing experiences. This social layer is a major differentiator and must remain visible throughout the site.

Avoid a generic grid of noisy feature cards. The visual treatment should feel editorial and product-led.

### 7.6 Real product showcase

The purpose of this section is evidence: visitors should see that TradeHub is a functioning product.

Show authentic views such as:

- dashboard and market overview;
- company or instrument discovery;
- portfolio overview and allocation;
- transaction or investing-practice flow;
- community feed or discussion; and
- leaderboard or related engagement functionality, if still part of the current product.

Guidelines:

- Use real product captures, not recreated mockups.
- Redact or replace private user data.
- Clearly distinguish simulated balances and positions.
- Optimize every image for the web.
- Give every meaningful image useful alternative text.
- Avoid auto-playing video.
- A lightweight tabbed or stepped showcase is acceptable only if it remains accessible and valuable when JavaScript is unavailable.
- Do not make the whole product understandable only through a carousel.

## 9. Tone and writing rules

The copy should be:

- clear;
- confident;
- factual;
- direct;
- accessible;
- specific to Morocco; and
- free from hype.

Prefer plain English. Avoid jargon, inflated financial language, and unverified superlatives.

### Do not use claims such as

- “Morocco's number-one investing platform”;
- “the future of investing”;
- “invest like a professional”;
- “your gateway to the Casablanca Stock Exchange”;
- “trusted by” followed by institutional logos;
- “real-time official market data” unless verified and approved;
- “buy Moroccan stocks” if the context could imply real-money execution; or
- any guaranteed-return, outperformance, or investment-success language.

### Terminology

- Use **virtual investing**, **investing practice**, **simulated balance**, and **simulated positions** when referring to current practice functionality.
- Use **platform**, **experience**, or **environment** for TradeHub as a whole.
- Use **Morocco-first** to signal focus, not exclusivity or official status.
- Refer to the **Casablanca Stock Exchange** only as market context or in the exact verified competition name. Never imply affiliation.

## 10. Regulatory, factual, and reputational guardrails

Never claim or imply that TradeHub:

- executes real-money investments today;
- is a broker;
- is licensed;
- is a regulated financial institution;
- has regulatory approval unless verified;
- is affiliated with or endorsed by the Bourse de Casablanca;
- is authorized to redistribute market data unless verified;
- is endorsed by CIH Bank, UM6P, StartGate, 1337, or another institution;
- provides personalized investment advice; or
- guarantees financial outcomes.

Do not fabricate:

- users or customer counts;
- assets under management;
- trading volume;
- waitlist size;
- growth figures;
- testimonials;
- partnerships;
- institutional relationships;
- performance metrics;
- launch dates; or
- product capabilities.

Every claim must be traceable to approved information in this brief or new wording explicitly approved by the founders.
